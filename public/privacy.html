Privacy

The short, human version

The contact form is the only thing on this site that collects information. What you type — your name, and optionally a phone number and an email address, plus your message — is delivered to Wild Aster so the shop can answer you. It is used for nothing else: no lists, no advertising, no resale.

This site loads no trackers, no cookies, and no third-party scripts. The stylesheet and every graphic on it come from this site itself.

Want your message deleted? Email hello@shopsaltandash.com and ask.
