About

Ashton Boehman — printmaker, crafter, shop owner

Wild Aster is named for the purple, star-shaped wildflower. Its owner is the person behind Salt & Ash Market, and the boutique is her second storefront.

Ashton grew up in St. Meinrad, Indiana, and moved to Indianapolis in 2014 to study printmaking at the Herron School of Art and Design at IU Indianapolis. She is a crafter first — the handmade sensibility runs through everything she stocks, and through the window displays she designs herself.

In 2024, while Salt & Ash Market was located in Fountain Square, she put a few clothing items on its racks. Customers were surprised there was so much apparel — and that it was size-inclusive. The clothing kept growing until it became its own leg of Salt & Ash. Wild Aster is where that leg becomes a store of its own: “a sister store, not a twin.”

Why Irvington? A small-town feel inside a big city, walkable neighbors like Hampton & Co. and The Med, and the East Washington Street corridor where the IndyGo Blue Line is under construction. And a storefront with history: the space Wild Aster takes over used to house Josephine’s, a local clothing store. “It was a space for people to go and feel confident, happy and I want to continue that.”

“I want to be that place people go on a walk and always stop in to see what’s new. I want people to go back to going to stores and that spirit of discovery.”

— Ashton Boehman · all quotes: Mirror Indy

Wild Aster & Salt & Ash

Same owner, same handmade heart — different shops. Salt & Ash Market is boutique bath-and-body in the Factory Arts District; Wild Aster is the clothing sister, opening in downtown Irvington.
