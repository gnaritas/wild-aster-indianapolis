Size-inclusive style lands in downtown Irvington.

Casual pieces like sweaters and stretchy pants, a few dresses for the occasions that matter — sized small to 3XL, priced under $100, with locally made accessories and a real focus on women-owned brands. Wild Aster opens Small Business Saturday, November 28, in the old Josephine’s storefront.

What’s coming through the door

Clothing that actually fits

Sweaters, stretchy pants, and a few occasion dresses — a full rack sized small to 3XL, chosen by a plus-sized owner who got tired of hunting for boutique pieces that fit.

Everything under $100

Every item on the racks is priced under $100. Looking good should not require a loan.

Women-owned brands

The buying philosophy is simple: Ashton focuses on sourcing from women-owned brands.

Locally made accessories

Accessories from local makers — A Knot That Binds and Rose and Frankie Jewelry — right alongside the clothing.

A sister store, not a twin

“It’s a sister store, not a twin,” says owner Ashton Boehman — Wild Aster is the clothing companion to Salt & Ash Market, her bath-and-body shop in the Factory Arts District. The apparel started in 2024 as a few racks inside Salt & Ash (back when it was in Fountain Square); customers loved that it was size-inclusive, and it grew into its own leg.

— Ashton Boehman, owner · source: Mirror Indy

The storefront

The old Josephine’s space, carried forward

Wild Aster takes over the former Josephine’s — a local clothing store — right in the heart of Irvington. It is the third women-led chapter for that storefront, and Ashton wants it to stay one: “It was a space for people to go and feel confident, happy and I want to continue that.”

Expect window displays worth a slow walk — they are where Ashton spends a lot of her creative energy.

Questions people are already asking

What sizes does Wild Aster carry?

Sizes run from small to 3XL. The racks hold casual pieces like sweaters and stretchy pants, plus a few dresses for special occasions.

How much will things cost?

Items are priced under $100.

When do you open, and what are your hours?

Wild Aster opens Saturday, November 28, 2026 — Small Business Saturday. Hours are 11 a.m. to 6 p.m., Wednesday through Sunday.

Yes. Locally made accessories from brands like A Knot That Binds and Rose and Frankie Jewelry sit alongside the clothing, and the focus is on sourcing from women-owned brands.

Downtown Irvington on Indianapolis's east side — the former Josephine's storefront along the East Washington Street corridor.
